# Color Update Guide

I've updated the color scheme to premium beige tones. Here's what changed:

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: `#b8a689` (beige-500)
- **Secondary**: `#d9a063` (cream-600) 
- **Accent**: `#e5b047` (gold-500)
- **Warm**: `#e5ba87` (cream-500)
- **Premium**: `#cbbda5` (beige-400)

## Text Colors
- **Primary Text**: `text-beige-900` (#2a241f) - for dark text on light backgrounds
- **Secondary Text**: `text-beige-700` - for medium contrast
- **Light Text**: `text-beige-200` - for light text on dark backgrounds

## Background Colors
- **Light BG**: `bg-beige-100` or `bg-beige-50`
- **Medium BG**: `bg-beige-200`
- **Dark BG**: `bg-beige-900` or `bg-beige-800`

## Gradients
- **Warm Gradient**: `warm-gradient` - from cream to gold
- **Premium Gradient**: `premium-gradient` - beige to cream
- **Beige Gradient**: `beige-gradient` - light beige tones

All pages have been updated to use these premium colors that match beautifully with the beige background!
